Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lumière Glow ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our website and Pinterest content curation activities.

1. Who We Are

Lumière Glow is an independent beauty content curation brand based in Austria. We share skincare and beauty product recommendations on Pinterest. Contact: lumiereglow.info@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

This website does not collect any personal data from visitors. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools on this website.

If you contact us by email, we will only use your email address to respond to your inquiry and will not share it with third parties.

3. Pinterest Integration

We use the Pinterest API to publish content (images and descriptions) to our own Pinterest business account. This integration:

  • Only posts content to our own Pinterest boards
  • Does not collect or store any data from Pinterest users
  • Does not access any Pinterest user accounts other than our own
  • Is used solely for content publishing purposes

4. Affiliate Links

Some links in our Pinterest content are affiliate links, primarily through the Amazon Associates program and Awin. This means we may earn a small commission if you purchase a product through our link, at no additional cost to you. We always disclose affiliate links with #Werbung or *Affiliate Link.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Pinterest profile is subject to Pinterest's own Privacy Policy, available at policy.pinterest.com. Product links may lead to Amazon.de or other retailers, each with their own privacy policies.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

As we are based in Austria (EU), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not collect personal data through this website, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. For any questions, contact us at lumiereglow.info@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions: lumiereglow.info@gmail.com
